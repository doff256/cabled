# R12: device and app matrix, colour per app

R12 is driven by testing: you try Cabled Camera in the apps people really use, and I fix what turns up. This page is the matrix to fill in.

## Built so far

- **Colour on the iPhone (found by audit):**
  - iOS switches the camera to Display P3 (wide colour) wherever a format allows. The encoder only *labels* frames BT.709; it doesn't convert them. So P3 colours could reach the PC mislabelled, giving slightly dull reds and skin tones in every app.
  - The phone now turns that off (`automaticallyConfiguresCaptureDeviceForWideColor = false`) and sets the camera to BT.709 (`activeColorSpace = .sRGB`) on every start and format change.
  - The phone reports its colour space. Diagnostics shows it as "Colour: camera rec709".
- **Colour on Windows (checked, already right):** the camera's formats carry BT.709, limited range, BT.709 primaries and transfer. The engine converts with BT.709. RGB32 is full range.
- **Reference image:** [assets/colour-bars-reference.png](assets/colour-bars-reference.png) shows how the fake phone's colour bars should look in an app that reads the tags correctly.
- **iPhone build:** 12.

## How to test

**Colour check (no iPhone needed):**
1. Quit Cabled from the tray. Two engines would fight over the camera.
2. Double-click `windows\scripts\demo-fakephone.cmd`.
3. In each app, pick Cabled Camera. Put its preview next to the reference image, at full brightness on the same screen.
4. If an app reads the video as BT.601 instead, it shows a yellower green, a bluer magenta and a greener cyan.

Stop the demo with Ctrl+C, and close the "Cabled fake phone" window.

**With the iPhone:** use the normal installed Cabled. Open Diagnostics and check that Colour reads `rec709`. Then check each app below.
