# R9: full app UI, image adjustments, presets, tray

## What was built

- **Side panel with pages.** Settings sit next to the preview, so every change is visible as it's made.
  - **Framing:** the box-in-box, zoom, speed and Everyone.
  - **Camera:** focus, exposure, white balance and the quality pins.
  - **Image:** the adjustments below.
  - **Presets:** saved combinations of settings.
  - **App:** login, tray and about.
- **Image adjustments.** These run on the PC, in `windows/src/engine/image_adjust.*`.
  - Brightness, contrast, saturation, sharpness and mirror.
  - They apply to what every app receives, and to the preview, which still shows exactly what apps get.
  - When everything is neutral the pass is skipped and costs nothing.
  - Sharpening works on NV12, the format apps normally pick. YUY2 and RGB32 get everything except sharpening.
  - With mirror on, a click on the preview is flipped back, so tap-to-focus still lands on the right spot.
- **Rotation override.** A new CONTROL_SET field, `rotation`: 0, 90, 180, 270 or `"auto"`. Use it for a phone lying flat or on an odd mount, where iOS auto-rotation guesses wrong.
- **Per-iPhone settings.** Stored in `%APPDATA%\Cabled\devices\<id>.json`.
  - Framing, camera, image, quality and the chosen lens are saved a second after each change, and restored when that phone connects.
  - A file that can't be read is renamed to `.bad`, and defaults are used.
  - App settings go in `settings.json`.
- **Presets.** These belong to each iPhone.
  - A preset keeps framing, camera and image settings together.
  - You can save, apply (or double-click), rename and delete them.
- **Tray and start at login (D-20).**
  - Closing the window keeps Cabled running in the tray; this is an App setting, on by default. Quit is in the tray's right-click menu.
  - "Start at login, in the tray" adds or removes an HKCU Run entry (`Cabled.exe --tray`). It shows its real state; onboarding in R11 will offer it ticked by default.
  - The tray is plain Win32, with no new dependency.
- **One copy only.** Launching Cabled again shows the running copy.
- **App icon** (placeholder): `app/Cabled.App/Assets/Cabled.ico`.
- **iPhone build:** 11, for the rotation override.

Diagnostics and onboarding stay in R11. The tray icon, single instance and `--tray` start were checked against `cabled-fakephone`. All 78 Windows tests pass, including 6 new image-adjustment tests.

## Owner test checklist (iPhone build 11 + new Cabled.exe)

1. **Pages:** open Cabled. The side panel has five tabs, and the preview stays visible on all of them.
2. **Image:**
   - Move brightness, contrast, saturation and sharpness. The preview and the camera in another app (OBS or Camera) should change together.
   - Reset to neutral should put everything back.
3. **Mirror:** turn it on. The picture flips in other apps too. Clicking the preview still focuses on the spot you clicked.
4. **Rotation:** try each option with the phone upright. "Auto" follows the phone again.
5. **Settings are remembered:** change a few things, quit from the tray, open Cabled again and connect the phone. Everything comes back.
6. **Presets:**
   - Save "Desk", change things, save "Evening".
   - Apply each; the picture should switch.
   - Rename one and delete one.
7. **Tray:**
   - Close the window while OBS shows Cabled Camera. The video keeps going and the icon stays in the tray.
   - Left-click the tray icon to open the window. Right-click and choose Quit to exit.
8. **One copy only:** with Cabled in the tray, start it again from Explorer. The existing window should appear; a second copy should not start.
9. **Start at login:** turn it on, sign out and back in. Cabled should be in the tray, and plugging in the phone makes the camera work.
