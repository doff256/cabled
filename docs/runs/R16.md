# R16: iPhone app in Mangrove Glass

The companion's one screen used the design system for the first time. Until this run it was still the plain list from the R1 spike. Its job hasn't changed, and there are no protocol or Windows changes.

## What was built

- **`App/Mangrove.swift`:**
  - Dark tokens (the same values as `make-theme.ps1`), and Bricolage Grotesque, Geist and Geist Mono bundled and registered at launch. They scale with Dynamic Type.
  - Motion tokens: `ease-spring` / `dur-settle` and `ease-out` / `dur-move`.
  - Materials: carved well, solid card, the lacquered primary button, the ghost button and the status pill with its breathing dot.
  - **Glass is Apple's own:** `glassEffect` on iOS 26, `.ultraThinMaterial` before that. None of it is drawn here.
- **`App/IrisView.swift`:** the app icon's framing iris as a live shape, with the same geometry as `design/icon/make-icon.mjs`. Changes in how open it is land with a spring. While live, it drifts slowly over `dur-breathe`. It holds still under Reduce Motion.
- **`App/StatusView.swift`, one screen:**

  | State | Pill | Iris | Text |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Waiting | WAITING | closed | "Waiting for your PC", plug in and open Cabled on Windows |
  | Connected | CONNECTED (tide) | a third open | "Connected to your PC" |
  | Live | LIVE (glow, breathing) | open, drifting | "Streaming to your PC", plus a mono readout: output, frame rate, codec, phone heat |
  | Paused | PAUSED (clay) | half open | Reason-specific: camera in use, too hot, call, background, error |
  | Camera access off | CAMERA OFF (clay) | closed | "Open Settings" as the primary button |
  | Couldn't listen | ERROR (rose) | closed | Close and reopen |

  - **Signature card:** days left, turning clay under 2 days. The bar then offers "Open SideStore" (`sidestore://`).
  - **Glass bar:** version, protocol and **Details**. Details is a sheet with the link test, the PC's version, pings and the last 60 log lines.
  - One entrance animation: the blocks rise 8 pt and fade in, 30 ms apart.
- **Plumbing:** `CompanionEvent.streaming` now carries `StreamInfo` (size, fps, codec) instead of a text line. A new `.camera(interruption:)` event surfaces AVCaptureSession interruptions on screen. The R1 "Listen on" picker is gone (it's always loopback).

## Not done

## Owner checks

1. Install the `Cabled-ipa` from this run's CI with SideStore and open it. The blocks should rise in once, and the iris should be nearly closed with "Waiting for your PC".
2. Plug into the PC with Cabled open on Windows. The screen should show CONNECTED, then LIVE: the iris springs open and drifts, the dot breathes, and the readout shows the real size and frame rate.
3. Open FaceTime while streaming, then go back to Cabled. It should show PAUSED, "Camera in use by another app", and recover by itself.
4. Tap **Details**. A glass sheet should open with the log.
5. With Reduce Motion on (Settings → Accessibility → Motion), nothing should drift or breathe.
6. Screenshots of the waiting and live states, please.
