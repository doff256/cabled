You need: this PC (Apple Devices is already installed), the iPhone Air and/or iPhone 17 Pro, and either your Mac (Xcode) or SideStore.

>
> **Was blocked, now unblocked (2026-11-21):** SideStore's Apple ID sign-in failed for everyone after Apple changed its sign-in servers on 2026-11-20 (HTTP 503 at the GSA/SPIM endpoint, then ADI -45063 / HTTP 401 on retries). Please do both on at least one phone eventually. The SideStore path also checks the signing-expiry readout.

1. **Trust:** did `list` show the phone *before* you tapped "Trust This Computer"? Did `test` connect before trusting? (Only the first time a phone meets this PC can show this. Don't reset trust settings on purpose: that also resets location and privacy permissions.)

6. **Background:** with `test` running, swipe to the home screen. What does the probe say? Reopen the app and run `test` again. Does it reconnect without replugging?

**Bug found (build 1):** only the first PC connection per app launch worked. Later ones were closed the instant they arrived ("PC connected" then "PC disconnected", no HELLO). Cause: when a connection ended, its socket number was freed, the next accepted connection usually got the same number, and the server's "close the previous connection" then shut down the *new* socket. Fixed in build 2 (`Connection.close()` no longer touches a socket it has already closed).

**Verdict so far:** D-01 confirmed on a real iPhone: usbmux reaches an app listening on loopback with no Local Network prompt, and 315 Mbit/s is ~3× what 4K60 H.264 at 100 Mbit/s needs (70% budget = 220 Mbit/s).

## Decision impact
- Loopback works, no prompt → D-01 confirmed. The companion binds to loopback only, and the bind switch is removed in R3.
- Only "All interfaces" works → companion binds to all interfaces. Document the Local Network prompt in onboarding and re-check R-60 (we still never *use* Wi-Fi).
- Throughput below ~60 Mbit/s → 4K30 H.264 doesn't fit, and HEVC becomes the default on that phone (Negotiator in R5).
