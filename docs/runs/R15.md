# R15: Mangrove Glass look

The Windows app now follows the owner's design system, Mangrove Glass. No engine, protocol or iPhone changes were made.

The first attempt only recoloured WinUI's stock controls, and the owner rejected it: the pill-shaped lens buttons and the stock switches and sliders didn't look like the gallery. This version ports the system's own components from its source:
- `bundle.css`
- `components/Window.html`, `Slider.html` and `Readout.html`

The headless-Edge screenshots of the gallery served as the reference.

## What was built

- **Theme** (`app/Cabled.App/Themes/`):
  - `make-theme.ps1` writes `Mangrove.xaml` from the tokens (dark, light, high contrast).
  - It adds the gradients the materials need: lacquer, lacquer shine, the toggle track, the knob and thumb, the well shade, the glass sheen.
  - The control templates live in `Mangrove.static.xaml`. Each is a port of a `bundle.css` class:

    | Template | Port of | What it does |
    |---|---|---|
    | `Button` (implicit) | `mg-btn` | Raised `surface-300`, top edge light, drop shadow. Sinks 1 px into a well when pressed, brightens on hover |
    | `AccentButtonStyle` | `mg-btn--primary` | Lacquered mangrove with the glass highlight on its top half |
    | `GlassButtonStyle` | glass variant, 30 px | Used over the preview |
    | `GhostButtonStyle` | `mg-btn--ghost` | |
    | `DangerButtonStyle` | `mg-btn--danger` | Rose text |
    | `LensToggleStyle` | Window.html lens picker | Glass buttons; the selected lens is the primary |
    | `ToggleSwitch` (implicit) | `mg-toggle` | 44×26 carved track, 20 px gradient knob that stretches while held and lands with a spring. The label sits to the right and is the toggle's `Header` |
    | `Slider` (implicit) | `components/Slider.html` | 10 px carved groove, fill inset 2 px (`mangrove-deep` to `mangrove`, glows while dragging), 22 px raised thumb that grows 18% |
    | `PillStyle` | `mg-pill` | The word and the dot both take the status colour |

  - Also `CardStyle` (`mg-solid`, padding 14), `GlassPanelStyle` (`mg-glass`, in-app acrylic) and the type scale.
- **Main window**, laid out as in `components/Window.html`, which is the Cabled mock:
  - **Title bar:** 46 px, with the lens-and-cable line glyph, "Cabled" and the status pill. The pill reads LIVE (breathing), CONNECTED, CONNECTING, PAUSED, OPEN THE APP, SETUP NEEDED, NO IPHONE or ERROR.
  - **Preview:** carved in. A glass flyout sits bottom-left with "Lens" (or the lens name) over the lens buttons, plus "Zoom" with glass preset buttons, the slider and the mono value.
  - **Side column:** the page tabs, then the window's one sigil, then the settings in solid cards. Toggles read "Auto-framing", "Auto focus", "Auto exposure", "Auto white balance", "Mirror", and so on.
  - **Under the preview:** a **Readout** (`components/Readout.html`). Latency is the hero number, and output, frame rate, bitrate and Cabled Camera are mono rows with hairlines. (A frame-rate Gauge was tried and dropped: frame rate barely moves, so a needle adds nothing.)
  - Empty state: the Cabled glyph with a broken cable and "No video yet".
  - No yellow anywhere: the click-to-focus square and the framing box are mangrove-glow.
- **Fixes after the first owner test:**
  - Switches didn't toggle, because the template lacked the `SwitchThumb` part that WinUI's ToggleSwitch listens to. It now has `SwitchThumb`, `SwitchKnob`, `SwitchKnobBounds` and `KnobTranslateTransform`, and a click on the label toggles too.
  - The zoom slider was jittery: its fill used a negative margin that knocked the thumb 9 px off the pointer, and the phone's zoom reports moved the thumb while it was held. A held slider is now left alone.
  - Preset buttons (2×…) move the slider straight away, gliding there with `ease-spring` over `dur-settle`, and so does the phone's own zoom. It snaps when Windows animations are off.
- **Crash log:** a startup or unhandled error is now written to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Cabled\crash.log`. Before, a XAML error closed the app with no trace.
- **Installer:** `build-installer.ps1` copies compiled XAML from subfolders (`Themes\Mangrove.xbf`) and checks that the theme and fonts are packaged.

## App icon: framing iris

Four aperture blades close onto a square opening, which is the box auto-framing draws, with a subject dot inside. Cream on mangrove lacquer. The design doesn't depend on the "Cabled" name.

- **Source:** `design/icon/make-icon.mjs` (run with `node`). It writes:
  - **iOS:** `ios/AppIcon.icon`, an Icon Composer package with three layers (blades A, blades B at 60%, subject) over a gradient fill.
    - We follow Apple's rules: flat, unmasked layers and no drawn highlights or shadows. iOS 26 adds the Liquid Glass.
    - Xcode generates flat icons from the same package for iOS 17 and 18.
    - The package was written by hand because Icon Composer needs macOS Tahoe. Xcode 26 compiles it.
  - **Windows:** a flat version of the same art, `design/icon/out/flat.svg`. `design/icon/make-ico.ps1` renders it with headless Edge at sizes from 16 to 256 px into `Assets/Cabled.ico` (used by the app and the installer).
- **CI:** iOS builds now run on `macos-26` (Xcode 26.6), because Xcode 16.4 can't compile `.icon` packages. `ios.yml` checks that the built app carries `Assets.car` and `CFBundleIcons`.
- The tray icon and the title-bar glyph still show the old lens-and-cable design.
